brainrot — ASCII mascot

An original pixel creature: a cracked-open skull with spore-green brain shoots,
mismatched eyes, a stitched mouth, and three uneven legs. Pure ASCII (no
Unicode), so it renders identically in every terminal, CI log and SSH session.

Render it in green:  ./scripts/banner.sh

Vector versions: assets/mascot.svg, assets/mascot-mark.svg, assets/banner.svg
